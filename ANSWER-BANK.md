## What the business does (short)
Juniper Lane Studio builds booking and loyalty apps for independent fitness studios. Each studio gets its own app under its own App Store account, owns it, and pays a flat monthly fee for upkeep instead of renting a white-label platform.

## The problem / why I started
I taught spin classes through college and watched the studio owner run bookings through Instagram DMs and a spreadsheet. The platforms that fix that charge a percentage of every class pass, forever. I study business, so the math bothered me: a small studio pays more in platform fees in two years than a custom app costs to build.

## Customer / segment
Independent fitness studios with one or two locations and 150 to 600 members, starting in the Durham and Raleigh area.

## Team
Sam Rivera builds every app and runs sales. Alex Chen handles design and onboarding part-time.

## Competition / differentiation
Booking platforms charge monthly fees plus a cut of sales, and the studio never owns the app. Agencies quote five figures. We charge $2,500 plus $120 a month, cancel any time and keep everything.

## Traction (verify against BUSINESS.md before use)
- $18,400 revenue in the last 12 months.
- 9 paying studios, 3 on a monthly plan.
- No outside capital, 0 employees.

## Biggest challenge / risk
Sales, not building. Most customers came from referrals, and we don't yet have a repeatable outbound process.

## Use of funds (scale to the award)
Test devices, developer and hosting costs, and a booth at the regional fitness owners' expo. 90-day goal: ten demos, four new studios.
